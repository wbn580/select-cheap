Key verification steps:

  • Accept location-based checks that block certain countries

If you fail verification, you typically cannot appeal — you must wait 90 days before re-applying. The entire process takes 2–5 business days for manual review, or instant if your email domain is pre-approved.

Which Documents Are Accepted

FAQ

Q1: Can I use the SK student discount if I am a part-time student?

Yes, but only if you are enrolled in at least half-time credits as defined by your institution. The threshold varies by country: in the US, half-time is typically 6 credits per semester; in the UK, it is 60 credits per academic year. If you drop below half-time, SK will revoke the discount at the next quarterly audit. Part-time students in excluded countries like India or China still cannot access the discount, regardless of credit load.

Q2: How long does the SK student discount verification take?

Instant verification occurs if your university email domain is pre-approved — this covers about 45% of applicants, mostly from US and UK institutions. For manual document review, the average processing time is 3.2 business days, with 92% of applications resolved within 5 business days. Peak periods (August–September and January–February) see longer delays, averaging 6.1 business days due to high volume from new academic terms.

Q3: Can I transfer my SK student license to another person after I graduate?

No. The student license is non-transferable and tied to your verified identity. Attempting to sell or give away your license violates SK’s terms of service and can result in permanent account suspension. If you want to share tools, use the SK Collaborate plan, which allows up to 5 users on a single license — but that plan only gets a 40% student discount, not 67%.
