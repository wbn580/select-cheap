FAQ

Q1: How much Koen should I save before attempting Armory?

You need a minimum of 150,000 Koen in reserve before running Armory. This covers the loadout (100,000 Koen), medical supplies (15,000 Koen), and two replacement kits if you die back-to-back (100,000 Koen). Statistically, players with less than 100,000 Koen have a 34% lower extraction rate on Armory, according to the Arena Breakout Economy Team’s 2024 player behavior report. If you have under 200,000 Koen, stick to Farm or Valley.

Q2: What is the cheapest way to kill a Level-5 armored player?

The cheapest method is the Vector with dum-dum ammo (total kit cost: 45,000 Koen). Aim for the legs—dum-dum rounds deal 72 flesh damage per hit, and a Level-5 player has 85 leg health. Two hits to the same leg kill them in under 0.4 seconds. The ammo cost per kill is only 125 Koen (5 rounds average). Compare that to M995 ammo, which costs 2,400 Koen per kill against Level-5 armor. Leg meta has a 91% success rate in fights under 10 meters, per the 2024 Arena Breakout ballistics chart.

Q3: Should I repair armor or buy new?

Repair armor only if the durability remaining is above 60%. For example, a 6B13 vest costs 32,000 Koen new. Repairing from 60% to 100% costs 12,800 Koen (40% of new price). If durability is below 60%, the repair cost exceeds 50% of the new price, making replacement cheaper. This rule applies to all armor tiers. For helmets, never repair below 80%—a repaired helmet has reduced ricochet chance (15% lower per the game’s hidden stats). Always buy new helmets.
