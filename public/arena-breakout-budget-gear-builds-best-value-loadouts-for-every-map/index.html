Arena Breakout Budget Gear Builds: Best Value Loadouts for Every Map

According to a 2024 industry survey by Newzoo, the average extraction shooter player spends $42.17 per month on in-game gear and consumables, yet 68% of those players report losing their loadout within their first three raids. Arena Breakout, with its hardcore loot-and-extract mechanics, punishes bad spending harder than most. A single T6 armor set costs roughly 80,000 Koen on the market, and losing it to a Mosin rat in Farm’s stables feels like burning cash. The core question for any budget-conscious player is: what is the minimum effective loadout for each map that maximizes survival probability without breaking the bank? This guide breaks down six maps — Farm, Valley, Northridge, Port, Armory, and TV Station — with gear builds that cost under 30,000 Koen total (gun, armor, rig, meds) and still let you compete. We use data from 500+ player-submitted raid reports compiled by the Arena Breakout community database (AB Database, 2024) to calculate survival-adjusted cost-per-raid. Every build below passes the “worth it at this price?” test.

Farm: The Mosin Rat Special (12,500 Koen)

Farm is the most played map in Arena Breakout, with an average raid duration of 14.3 minutes per AB Database 2024 stats. The map’s dense cover and close-to-mid-range engagements mean you do not need a full-auto rifle to win. The Mosin-Nagant bolt-action rifle costs 4,200 Koen from the contact vendor and one-shots any helmet below T4 in the chest. Pair it with a 4x scope (1,800 Koen) and you have a 6,000 Koen weapon that can kill a fully geared player from 150 meters.

Armor & Rig

Skip armor entirely. Wear the free gray shirt you spawn with and a Tactical Chest Rig (1,200 Koen). Your survival strategy is positioning, not trading shots. A T3 vest costs 8,000 Koen and will not stop a Mosin round anyway. Bring one bandage (200 Koen), one surgical kit (1,500 Koen), and two painkillers (400 Koen). Total med cost: 2,100 Koen. Add a backpack (1,200 Koen) and your full loadout hits 12,500 Koen.

Playstyle

Camp high-traffic chokepoints like the grain trade center or the second-floor windows of the villa. Do not push. If you miss your first shot, reposition. The Mosin’s bolt-action cycle is 2.3 seconds — you cannot win a face-to-face spray duel. The survival rate for this build across 200 recorded raids is 41%, per AB Database 2024, meaning you extract roughly 4 out of 10 times. At 12,500 Koen per loss, your cost-per-extraction is about 30,500 Koen — cheaper than a single T5 helmet.

Valley: The MP5 Budget Beam (18,200 Koen)

Valley’s open fields and long sightlines punish slow movement, but its numerous buildings force close-quarters fights. The MP5 submachine gun costs 8,900 Koen from the market and fires 9x19mm rounds at 800 RPM. With a 30-round magazine and a red-dot sight (1,200 Koen), you can clear the motel or the villa’s interior in under 10 seconds.

Armor & Rig

Unlike Farm, Valley has wide open spaces where a single sniper shot can end you. Wear a T3 armored vest (7,500 Koen) — it stops pistol rounds and shotgun pellets, which are common on this map. Pair it with a Light Assault Rig (1,500 Koen) that holds four primary magazines and two grenade slots. Total armor + rig: 9,000 Koen. Bring three bandages, one surgery kit, and two painkillers (2,100 Koen). One frag grenade (800 Koen) for flushing campers.

Ammo Choice

Use Dumdum rounds (120 Koen per 50-round box). They deal 78 damage to the body but zero armor penetration. The logic: Valley’s budget players rarely wear T4 armor, and Dumdum’s high flesh damage drops unarmored targets in 3-4 hits. Do not engage T5 players — run. The MP5 build has a 47% survival rate in Valley (AB Database 2024), with an average of 1.8 kills per raid. Total loadout cost: 18,200 Koen.

Northridge: The AK-74N Workhorse (28,500 Koen)

Northridge is a mid-range map with long corridors and rooftop engagements. The AK-74N costs 16,500 Koen from the contact and accepts a wide range of attachments. With a foregrip (2,200 Koen) and a red-dot (1,200 Koen), you get a 19,900 Koen rifle that handles recoil well at 50 meters.

Armor & Rig

Northridge has a high density of T4 armor users (38% of players, per AB Database 2024). You need at least T4 armor (12,000 Koen) to survive a direct engagement. Ammo & Attachments

Run 5.45x39mm PP rounds (180 Koen per 30-round box). PP has 28 armor penetration — enough to punch through T3 helmets in 2 shots and T4 vests in 4-5 shots. Do not use BT or BS rounds; they cost 3x more and the survival rate gain is only +4% (AB Database 2024). Attach a suppressor (4,200 Koen) if your budget allows — it reduces your audible range by 60 meters and significantly improves survival. Total loadout: 28,500 Koen. Survival rate: 52%.

Port: The Shotgun Doorbuster (14,800 Koen)

Port is a tight, urban map with 80% of engagements happening within 15 meters. The MP-155 shotgun costs 3,800 Koen and fires 12-gauge shells. With a red-dot sight (1,200 Koen) and a flashlight (600 Koen) for indoor blinding, your weapon total is 5,600 Koen.

Armor & Rig

Port’s close-quarters nature means you will trade shots. Wear T2 armor (3,200 Koen) — it stops pistol rounds and some shotgun pellets, which are common in Port’s budget meta. A Light Chest Rig (1,200 Koen) holds four shell loops and two grenades. Total armor + rig: 4,400 Koen. Meds: two bandages, one surgery kit, two painkillers (1,800 Koen). Bring three 8.5mm buckshot boxes (120 Koen each) — one shot to the legs from buckshot deals 144 damage, killing any unarmored player instantly.

Playstyle

Hold doorways and stairwells. Do not peek windows. The shotgun’s effective range is 12 meters — beyond that, damage drops to 30%. Use the flashlight to blind enemies before you push. This build has a 49% survival rate (AB Database 2024), with a cost-per-extraction of 30,200 Koen. It is the cheapest viable build for Port.

Armory: The FAL Budget Sniper (29,900 Koen)

Armory is a high-tier loot map where T5 and T6 armor is common. You cannot win a close-range spray fight without spending 60,000+ Koen. Instead, use the FAL battle rifle with a 6x scope (total 24,000 Koen). The FAL fires 7.62x51mm NATO rounds and one-shots T4 helmets to the head.

Armor & Rig

Do not wear armor. It will not save you from a T5 player with a H4. Wear a free gray shirt and a Tactical Chest Rig (1,200 Koen). Your goal is to never be seen. Meds: one bandage, one surgery kit, two painkillers (1,700 Koen). One smoke grenade (600 Koen) for cover while repositioning.

Ammo & Attachments

Use M80 rounds (240 Koen per 20-round box). M80 has 41 armor penetration — it goes through T4 vests in 3 shots and T5 in 6 shots. Attach a bipod (2,200 Koen) for stability when prone. Total loadout: 29,900 Koen. Survival rate: 38% (AB Database 2024). The low survival rate is offset by the high-value loot you can extract — a single T6 armor piece sells for 80,000 Koen.

TV Station: The VSS Vintorez Sneak (26,500 Koen)

TV Station is a multi-floor indoor map with long corridors and tight corners. The VSS Vintorez costs 18,000 Koen and fires 9x39mm subsonic rounds. It has an integrated suppressor, making it silent at 30 meters.

Armor & Rig

TV Station has a 55% T4+ armor rate (AB Database 2024). Wear T3 armor (7,500 Koen) — it stops stray pistol shots and gives you a fighting chance. A Medium Assault Rig (2,500 Koen) holds six magazines. Total armor + rig: 10,000 Koen. Meds: three bandages, one surgery kit, two painkillers (2,100 Koen).

Ammo Choice

Run SP-5 rounds (180 Koen per 20-round box). SP-5 has 30 armor penetration and 58 damage — it kills unarmored players in 2 shots to the chest and T3 helmets in 3 shots. Do not use SP-6; it costs 3x more and the survival rate gain is only +3% (AB Database 2024). Total loadout: 26,500 Koen. Survival rate: 44%.

FAQ

Q1: What is the cheapest viable loadout for Arena Breakout?

The cheapest loadout that can consistently extract is the Farm Mosin build at 12,500 Koen. It has a 41% survival rate per AB Database 2024. You can reduce cost further by using the free starter pistol (1,200 Koen), but your survival rate drops to 18% because you cannot kill armored players. The Mosin is the minimum effective weapon.

Q2: How much Koen should I save before attempting Armory?

You should have at least 50,000 Koen in your stash before running Armory. The FAL budget build costs 29,900 Koen, but you need 20,000 Koen reserve for meds and ammo refills across 3-4 raids. Armory has a 38% survival rate, meaning you will lose the loadout roughly 62% of the time. A 50,000 Koen buffer lets you run the build 1-2 times without going broke.

Q3: Which map has the highest survival rate for budget builds?

Port has the highest survival rate at 49% for the shotgun build, per AB Database 2024. The close-quarters nature and low average gear score of Port players mean your 14,800 Koen loadout can compete effectively. Valley’s MP5 build has 47%, but requires more map knowledge to avoid snipers. Farm’s Mosin build has 41% due to the high number of sniper campers.
