Cheap Tools Online Australia: Shipping Cost Comparison and Return Policies

Online shoppers in Australia paid an average of AUD 12.70 per domestic parcel in 2024, according to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC)…

Online shoppers in Australia paid an average of AUD 12.70 per domestic parcel in 2024, according to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) 2024 Bulk Parcel Pricing Inquiry, while international shipping from major platforms like AliExpress and Amazon US often adds AUD 8–25 per order. With 63% of Australian consumers citing shipping costs as the primary reason for cart abandonment (Australia Post 2024 eCommerce Industry Report), the choice of cheap tool retailer can make or break a budget. This comparison covers four major online tool sellers serving Australia — Banggood, AliExpress, Amazon Australia, and Kogan — focusing on shipping fees, delivery times, and return policy strictness. We calculate the real cost-per-feature for each platform, answer whether a free-shipping deal is actually worth it, and flag which retailers have return policies that protect buyers. If you are a price-sensitive shopper aged 18–35, this guide will help you avoid paying more in shipping than the tool itself costs.

Shipping Cost Breakdown: What You Actually Pay

Shipping cost is the single largest variable when buying cheap tools online in Australia. The ACCC 2024 report found that 71% of Australian e-commerce orders under AUD 50 include a shipping fee that exceeds 20% of the item price. For tool shoppers, this means a AUD 15 screwdriver set can suddenly cost AUD 22 after shipping.

Banggood typically charges AUD 4.99–9.99 for standard shipping on tools under 1 kg, with free shipping on orders over AUD 49. Delivery takes 12–20 business days via Australia Post eParcel. AliExpress uses a tiered system: “Standard Shipping” starts at AUD 2.50 for small items like hex key sets, but rises to AUD 15–20 for power tools. Free shipping is common on items marked “Choice” when spending over AUD 25. Amazon Australia offers free shipping on orders over AUD 39 (non-Prime) or AUD 0 for Prime members (AUD 6.99/month for students). Delivery takes 3–8 business days for stocked items. Kogan provides free shipping on orders over AUD 49, with standard shipping at AUD 5.99 for smaller items, but tool delivery can take 7–14 business days.

The “worth it at this price?” test: a AUD 30 power drill from AliExpress with AUD 12 shipping (40% surcharge) is only worth it if the same drill costs over AUD 42 locally. For cross-border tuition payments or international purchases, some families use channels like Airwallex global account to settle fees with better exchange rates, though that’s a separate topic.

Express vs Standard: Time vs Cost

Express shipping on AliExpress costs AUD 15–30 and arrives in 7–12 business days via DHL or FedEx. Amazon Australia offers same-day or next-day delivery in metro areas for AUD 6.99–9.99. Banggood express via DHL costs AUD 12–18 and takes 7–10 business days. For a AUD 25 tool, express shipping at AUD 18 (72% of item price) is rarely worth it unless you need the tool urgently for a weekend project.

Return Policy Strictness: Who Protects You

Return policy terms vary dramatically between cheap tool retailers, and Australian Consumer Law (ACL) provides baseline protections. Under the ACL, consumers have the right to a refund or replacement for faulty goods regardless of the seller’s stated policy. However, international platforms often make returns impractical.

Amazon Australia has the most buyer-friendly return policy: 30-day returns on most tools, with free return shipping on faulty items. For change-of-mind returns, you pay return postage (typically AUD 7–15). Amazon also processes refunds within 3–5 business days after the item is received. Kogan offers a 30-day satisfaction guarantee, but you must pay return shipping (AUD 9.95 flat fee for standard items). Tools over AUD 100 require pre-approval and may incur a 15% restocking fee.

Banggood and AliExpress are stricter. Banggood requires customers to return items to a Chinese warehouse at their own cost (typically AUD 15–30 via tracked post). AliExpress offers buyer protection for 75 days from purchase, but refunds are often partial (50–80%) if the item is “used” or packaging is damaged. For tools under AUD 20, the return shipping cost exceeds the item value, making returns economically unfeasible.

The ACCC 2023–24 enforcement report noted that 34% of consumer complaints about online shopping involved international returns. If you buy a AUD 18 wrench set from AliExpress that arrives with a defect, the cheapest tracked return to China costs AUD 22 — you are better off accepting the loss.

Restocking Fees and Exceptions

Kogan charges a 15% restocking fee on returned power tools if the box is opened. Amazon Australia does not charge restocking fees for any category. Banggood charges a 10–20% restocking fee on “large tools” (drills, saws) if returned within 30 days. AliExpress does not have a formal restocking fee but may deduct 10–30% for “used condition” items.

Tool Categories: Where Each Platform Excels

Hand tools (wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers) are cheapest on AliExpress, with prices 40–60% below Australian retail. A 12-piece screwdriver set costs AUD 5–8 on AliExpress versus AUD 18–25 at Bunnings. Shipping adds AUD 3–5, so the total is still 50% cheaper. The trade-off is 14–25 day delivery and no practical return option.

Power tools (drills, grinders, saws) are best bought on Amazon Australia or Kogan. Amazon’s free shipping over AUD 39 and 30-day returns make a AUD 89 cordless drill a safer purchase than the same model on Banggood for AUD 72 plus AUD 12 shipping. The AUD 5 price difference is offset by Amazon’s return convenience and 3-day delivery.

Specialty tools (circuit testers, calipers, precision knives) are a mixed bag. Banggood has the widest selection of niche tools under AUD 30, but shipping takes 15–25 days. AliExpress often has identical items for 10–15% less, but with longer delivery estimates.

Battery-Powered Tools: Shipping Restrictions

Lithium-ion batteries face strict shipping regulations. Amazon Australia ships battery tools via ground transport only (5–8 business days). Banggood and AliExpress often require air-surcharge fees of AUD 8–15 for battery-containing items. Kogan ships battery tools only within Australia, with no additional fee. If you need a cordless drill, Kogan or Amazon are the practical choices.

Delivery Time vs Cost Trade-Off

Delivery time is the second most important factor after shipping cost. Australia Post data from 2024 shows that 78% of metro-area parcels arrive within 5 business days, but international orders take 14–28 days on average.

Amazon Australia (Prime): 1–3 business days. Non-Prime: 3–8 business days. This is the fastest option for most tools. Kogan: 5–10 business days for in-stock items, but “marketplace” sellers add 3–7 days. Banggood: 12–20 business days standard, 7–10 business days express. AliExpress: 14–25 business days standard, 7–12 business days express.

The cost-per-day calculation: AliExpress standard shipping at AUD 3.50 for a AUD 12 tool with 20-day delivery costs AUD 0.175 per day. Amazon Prime shipping at AUD 0 (included in membership) with 2-day delivery costs AUD 0 per day but requires a AUD 6.99/month commitment. For occasional tool buyers (1–2 orders per month), AliExpress standard shipping is cheaper. For frequent shoppers, Amazon Prime’s free shipping on 5+ orders per month saves AUD 25–40 compared to paying per-order shipping.

Tracking and Insurance

Amazon Australia provides real-time tracking with delivery notifications. Kogan uses Australia Post tracking and offers optional shipping insurance (AUD 2.99) for items over AUD 100. Banggood provides tracking numbers but updates are often delayed by 3–5 days. AliExpress tracking is reliable for express shipments but standard shipping tracking may stop updating once the parcel leaves China. For tools worth over AUD 50, choose a platform with full tracking.

Hidden Costs: Customs, GST, and Currency Fees

Hidden costs can add 10–30% to your tool purchase. Since July 1, 2018, all goods imported into Australia valued at AUD 1,000 or less are subject to 10% GST. Most platforms (Amazon, AliExpress, Banggood, Kogan) collect GST at checkout, so there are no surprise customs fees. However, items over AUD 1,000 incur additional customs processing fees (AUD 50–100) and potential import duties (5% for most tools).

Currency conversion fees apply when paying in foreign currencies. AliExpress and Banggood charge in AUD by default, but the exchange rate used is typically 2–4% worse than the mid-market rate. Amazon Australia charges in AUD with no conversion fee. Kogan charges in AUD. If you use a credit card with foreign transaction fees (2–3%), paying on AliExpress in USD can add AUD 1–3 to a AUD 30 tool.

Return shipping costs are the biggest hidden expense. As noted, returning a faulty AUD 25 tool to Banggood’s Chinese warehouse costs AUD 18–30. The ACCC 2024 report found that 41% of consumers who attempted an international return abandoned the process due to cost.

Deal or No Deal: Final Verdict by Scenario

Scenario 1: You need a cheap hand tool (under AUD 20) and can wait 2–3 weeks.
Deal: AliExpress with free shipping on Choice items. Total cost: AUD 8–15. Return risk is high, but at this price point, it’s an acceptable gamble.

Scenario 2: You need a power tool (AUD 50–150) and want returns protection.
Deal: Amazon Australia (non-Prime) with free shipping over AUD 39. Total cost: AUD 50–150. Returns are free for faults, and delivery takes 3–5 days.

Scenario 3: You need a specialty tool (AUD 10–30) and want it in under 10 days.
Deal: Kogan with AUD 5.99 shipping. Total cost: AUD 16–36. Return policy is decent, and delivery is 5–10 days.

Scenario 4: You are buying in bulk (5+ tools, AUD 100+ total).
Deal: Banggood with free shipping over AUD 49. Total cost: AUD 100–150. Shipping is free, but return policy is poor. Only buy if you are confident in product quality.

No deal: Any platform charging over 30% of item value in shipping, or any international platform for tools over AUD 100 where return shipping costs exceed the item’s value.

FAQ

Q1: What is the cheapest way to ship tools to Australia from overseas?

The cheapest shipping method is AliExpress “Standard Shipping” on Choice items, which is often free for orders over AUD 25. For a AUD 15 tool, shipping costs AUD 2.50–4.00, making the total 17–33% cheaper than Australian retail. Delivery takes 14–25 business days. Banggood’s free shipping threshold is higher (AUD 49), but their standard shipping starts at AUD 4.99. Amazon Australia’s free shipping over AUD 39 is the cheapest domestic option, with 3–8 day delivery.

Q2: Can I return a faulty tool to AliExpress or Banggood?

Yes, but it is often not worth the cost. AliExpress’s buyer protection covers faulty items for 75 days from purchase, but you must return the item to the seller’s Chinese warehouse at your own cost. Tracked return shipping from Australia to China costs AUD 18–30 for a 500g package. For tools under AUD 25, the return shipping exceeds the item’s value. Banggood similarly requires returns to China. Under Australian Consumer Law, international sellers are technically liable, but enforcement is difficult. For tools under AUD 30, accepting the loss is usually the practical choice.

Q3: Does Amazon Australia charge GST on tool purchases?

Yes, Amazon Australia charges 10% GST on all tool purchases at checkout, regardless of whether the item is sold by Amazon or a third-party marketplace seller. This is collected automatically and remitted to the Australian Taxation Office. There are no additional customs fees for items under AUD 1,000. For Prime members, GST is included in the displayed price. For non-Prime purchases, GST is added at checkout. The total price you see before payment includes GST for all domestic and international items sold on Amazon Australia.
