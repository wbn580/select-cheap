Free VPN Showdown: ProtonVPN, Windscribe, and Hide.me Compared for Budget Users

How the Free Plans Stack Up

For students and digital nomads watching every dollar, free VPN plans can look tempting. But not all are created equal. We compared the free tiers of ProtonVPN, Windscribe, and Hide.me based on our own speed tests, data allowances, privacy policies, and server coverage.

Speed and Performance

In our speed tests, ProtonVPN's free servers delivered consistent download speeds, though they were not the fastest. Windscribe's free plan offered higher peak speeds but varied by location. Hide.me's free servers were the slowest overall, but still usable for light browsing.

Data Limits and Usage

ProtonVPN's free plan provides unlimited data, but with no guarantee of speed. Windscribe gives you 10 GB per month on the free tier. Hide.me limits free users to 10 GB per month as well, but you can earn extra data by completing certain tasks.

Privacy and Logging

All three providers state a no-logs policy. ProtonVPN is based in Switzerland and has undergone independent audits. Windscribe is based in Canada and also claims a no-logs stance. Hide.me, based in Malaysia, similarly says it keeps no logs.

Server Coverage

ProtonVPN offers free servers in three countries: the US, Netherlands, and Japan. Windscribe's free plan includes servers in 10 countries, including the US, Canada, and Germany. Hide.me provides free servers in five locations: US, UK, Netherlands, Canada, and Germany.

Verdict

Your choice depends on priorities. If unlimited data matters most, ProtonVPN wins. If you want a wider server choice, Windscribe offers more free locations. Hide.me is a middle ground with reasonable data and a decent server spread.

FAQ

Are any of these free VPNs truly unlimited?

Only ProtonVPN offers unlimited data on its free plan.

Can I use these free plans for streaming?

We didn't test streaming specifically, but speeds may be variable.

How do I increase my data limit on Windscribe or Hide.me?

Windscribe gives a fixed 10 GB monthly. Hide.me lets you earn extra data by completing tasks.
