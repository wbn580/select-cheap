## Why a cheap VPN often isn't cheap

When shopping for a budget VPN, the advertised price is rarely the price you end up paying. Many providers advertise a low monthly rate but require a long-term commitment, and the actual monthly cost can be much higher after the initial term. Some also add hidden fees or automatically renew at a higher rate. For students and digital nomads watching every dollar, it pays to read the fine print before signing up.

## How we compared

We looked at 10 low-cost VPNs from the perspective of a budget-conscious global user. Our comparison covers four key areas: connection speed, privacy policy, ability to unlock streaming services, and the real monthly cost — not just the headline number. We also flagged common subscription traps, such as long-term contracts that auto-renew at a higher price.

## What we found

Here are the key takeaways from our comparison:

- **Speed varies widely** — some budget VPNs deliver near full-speed connections, while others can slow your internet dramatically.
- **Privacy policies differ** — some providers keep logs, while others have a strict no-logs policy. Check whether the provider is based in a jurisdiction that may compel data disclosure.
- **Streaming support is inconsistent** — not every budget VPN can reliably unblock Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, or other popular services. Some only work with certain platforms, and support can change.
- **Hidden fees are common** — several providers charge extra for features like dedicated IPs, or require payment for additional devices. Others have a money-back guarantee that only applies to the first term.
- **Long-term subscription traps** — the cheapest per-month price often locks you into a 2- or 3-year plan. If you cancel early, you may lose the discount or pay a penalty.

## How to avoid the hidden costs

To get a truly cheap and reliable VPN, follow these rules:

- Always calculate the real monthly cost over the full subscription term, including any renewal price increase.
- Read the privacy policy carefully — look for a clear no-logs statement and check the jurisdiction.
- Test the streaming service you care about before committing to a long plan.
- Watch for auto-renewal settings that silently raise your bill.
- Consider a provider that offers a short-term plan or a reliable money-back guarantee.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Are budget VPNs safe to use?**  
A: It depends on the provider. Some budget VPNs have strong privacy policies and encryption, while others may log your activity. Always verify the privacy policy.

**Q: Can I use a budget VPN to watch Netflix?**  
A: Some budget VPNs can unblock Netflix, but not all. Support can vary by service and change over time.

**Q: How can I tell the actual monthly cost?**  
A: Divide the total cost of the subscription by the number of months, and include any additional fees. Also check the renewal price.

**Q: What is a subscription trap?**  
A: A subscription trap often involves a low initial price that jumps after the first term, or a long-term contract that is expensive to cancel.

**Q: Should I choose a free VPN instead?**  
A: Free VPNs often have limitations, such as data caps and slower speeds. A low-cost paid VPN may offer better performance and privacy, but your choice should depend on your needs.

## Bottom line

The cheapest VPN is not always the best value. By focusing on real monthly cost, privacy, speed, and streaming support, you can find a budget VPN that works for you without falling into subscription traps.
