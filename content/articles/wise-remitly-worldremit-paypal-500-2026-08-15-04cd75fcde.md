## How the Main Services Stack Up for Small Transfers

For transfers under $500, the cheapest and fastest option depends on the corridor and the exact amount. Wise, Remitly, WorldRemit, and PayPal each have different fee models and exchange-rate markups. While Wise typically offers the mid-market exchange rate with a transparent fee, Remitly and WorldRemit often promote zero-fee or low-fee transfers to certain countries. PayPal generally charges a higher fee and a less favorable exchange rate, making it expensive for small transfers unless the recipient needs the PayPal ecosystem.

## Fee and Exchange Rate Comparison

- **Wise**: Uses the real mid-market exchange rate and charges a clear, percentage-based fee. For a $500 transfer, the fee is typically a few dollars, but the total cost is easily visible before sending.
- **Remitly**: Often advertises no transfer fee for first transfers or for certain corridors, but the exchange rate may include a markup. For small amounts, the fee might be $0, but the rate could be slightly worse than the mid-market rate.
- **WorldRemit**: Also has promotional zero-fee offers, but the exchange rate margin can vary. The total cost for a $500 transfer can be lower than PayPal but may not always beat Wise on total cost.
- **PayPal**: Charges a fixed fee plus a percentage (e.g., 5% + fixed fee) and uses a markup on the exchange rate. For a $500 transfer, the total cost is often significantly higher than the others, especially for cross-border payments.

## Which Is Fastest?

Speed varies by destination and payment method. Wise typically delivers within 1-2 business days, but sometimes within minutes for certain corridors. Remitly offers an "Express" option that can deliver in minutes for an extra fee, while the standard option takes a few days. WorldRemit also has instant delivery to mobile wallets or bank accounts in some countries. PayPal transfers are usually instant if the recipient has a PayPal account, but withdrawing to a bank can take 1-2 business days. Ultimately, the fastest option depends on the destination and the chosen delivery method.

## Country-Specific Suggestions

For transfers to the Philippines, Remitly often has low fees and fast delivery to local wallets like GCash. To India, Wise and Remitly both offer competitive rates, but Wise may be cheaper for larger amounts. For transfers to Mexico, WorldRemit and Remitly have strong corridors with frequent promotions. For Europe, Wise is often the cheapest due to the mid-market rate and low fees. Always check the exact fee and exchange rate at the time of transfer, as promotions and rates change frequently.

## FAQs

**Q: Is Wise always the cheapest for small transfers?**  
A: Not always. For some corridors, Remitly or WorldRemit may offer a $0 fee with a slightly worse exchange rate, which could still be cheaper than Wise for amounts under $500. Compare the total cost before sending.

**Q: How can I know the real exchange rate?**  
A: You can check the current mid-market rate on sites like XE or Google. The service's exchange rate is usually shown at checkout, so you can compare.

**Q: Which service has the fastest delivery?**  
A: It depends on the destination and delivery method. Remitly's Express and WorldRemit's instant options can be as fast as minutes, while Wise and PayPal often take a few hours to a couple of days.
