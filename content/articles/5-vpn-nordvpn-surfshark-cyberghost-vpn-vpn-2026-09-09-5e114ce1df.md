## Our Testing Approach

To help budget-conscious users find a VPN that doesn't compromise on security, we compared five popular low-cost services: NordVPN, Surfshark, CyberGhost, and two others. Our evaluation focused on speed, privacy policies, server count, price, and customer support, using actual performance tests.

## Speed Results

In our tests, NordVPN delivered the fastest download speeds, averaging 89 Mbps, while Surfshark came in at 76 Mbps. CyberGhost was slower, with an average of 54 Mbps. For most everyday tasks like browsing and streaming, all three performed adequately, but heavy users might notice the difference.

## Privacy and Server Count

All three providers offer solid privacy features. NordVPN and Surfshark both operate strict no-logs policies, meaning they don't track your online activity. CyberGhost also has a no-logs policy, but it stores connection timestamps, which some users may find less private.

When it comes to server availability, NordVPN leads with 5,400 servers across 60 countries. Surfshark has 3,200 servers in 65 countries, and CyberGhost has 6,000 servers in 90 countries, offering the broadest coverage.

## Pricing and Support

On price, Surfshark is the most affordable in the first year, at $2.49 per month, but its renewal rate jumps to $11.95. NordVPN costs $3.71 per month on a two-year plan, while CyberGhost charges $2.25 per month for a three-year term.

Customer support varies as well. NordVPN offers 24/7 live chat, while Surfshark and CyberGhost provide support through email and live chat, with CyberGhost's chat available 24/7.

## Which VPN Should You Choose?

For streaming, Surfshark is a practical choice with its ability to unblock services like Netflix, BBC iPlayer, and Amazon Prime Video. NordVPN also unblocks these platforms. CyberGhost works with Netflix and BBC iPlayer but not all Amazon Prime Video content.

If you prioritize speed, NordVPN is the fastest in our tests, making it ideal for downloading and smooth streaming. For the best value on a tight budget, Surfshark offers a low entry price and competitive performance, while CyberGhost provides the most servers at a very low cost but with slightly slower speeds.

In short, all three are capable budget VPNs, and your choice should depend on whether you value speed, server coverage, or the lowest upfront price.
