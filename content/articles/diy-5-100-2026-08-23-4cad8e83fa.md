## Overview

For DIY enthusiasts on a tight budget, finding a reliable screwdriver set under $100 can be a challenge. This comparison looks at five different sets, evaluating them on material, durability, handle design, and the number of included bits.

## Key Considerations

### Material and Durability

The material of the screwdriver bits and handle significantly affects the tool's lifespan. Sets made from hardened steel tend to withstand more torque and resist wear better than softer alloys. A durable handle material, such as rubberized plastic, provides a better grip and reduces hand fatigue during prolonged use.

### Handle Design

Ergonomic handles with textured grips improve control and reduce slipping, especially when applying force. Some designs feature a contoured shape that fits the hand naturally, making them more comfortable for extended tasks.

### Included Bits

The number and variety of included bits determine the set's versatility. A good set should cover common screw types like Phillips, flathead, and Torx, along with hex and star bits. More bits allow you to handle a wider range of projects without needing additional purchases.

## Buying Advice by Scenario

- **For general household repairs:** Choose a set that includes a variety of common bits and a comfortable handle.
- **For electronics and small appliances:** Look for precision bits and a set with a magnetic tip to hold tiny screws.
- **For furniture assembly:** A set with long-reaching bits and a ratcheting handle can speed up the process.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Overlooking bit quality:** Cheap bits may strip screws or break easily, so check for hardened steel construction.
- **Ignoring handle comfort:** A poor grip can lead to hand cramps and reduced control.
- **Buying based on bit count alone:** A set with many low-quality bits is less useful than a smaller set with durable, versatile options.

## Conclusion

When selecting a screwdriver set under $100, prioritize material durability, handle ergonomics, and bit variety. Match the set to your specific DIY needs to get the best value for your money.
